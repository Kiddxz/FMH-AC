Gender and Development (GAD)

Las Pinas National High School Almanza held a Gender and Development (GAD) program.

This program included seminars and workshops for teachers, staff, and students. Its goal is to teach everyone about equality, respect, and unity in school and society.

Brigada Cleaning Program of Lpnhs

Brigada Eskwela cleaning programs are vital because they directly foster a safe, clean, and conducive learning environment for students before the school year begins. By bringing together teachers, parents, students, and community volunteers, the initiative restores classroom facilities, removes health hazards, and reduces the risk of illness. Beyond physical maintenance, this nationwide effort instills a strong sense of civic duty and community ownership, ensuring that schools remain welcoming spaces where learners can thrive.
