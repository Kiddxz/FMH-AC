How to test synchronized calendars in XAMPP

1. Start Apache and MySQL, and keep your current supremedent_db database.
2. Login as a patient, complete your profile, open patient/appointments.php.
   Sunday boxes must be red / unclickable. Book a Monday–Saturday time.
3. On assistant/calendar.php or assistant/appointments.php, see the request
   labeled Pending. The calendar auto-refreshes every 30 seconds, on window
   focus, or through the Refresh button.
4. On dentist/appointments.php, the request should appear as Pending,
   explicitly unassigned, not a confirmed patient appointment.
5. Back in assistant/appointments.php, select a dentist and Approve.
6. The assistant and assigned dentist calendars should now say Approved.
   Other dentists no longer see this booking after it is assigned.
7. Click an approved booking on the dentist calendar to view patient details.
8. Verify assistant walk-ins and approvals cannot be scheduled on Sundays.

All 3 calendars use the existing appointments database table. No new schema
migration is needed. PHP lint and simulated calendar rendering were tested,
but database-backed XAMPP integration must be tested on your own environment.
